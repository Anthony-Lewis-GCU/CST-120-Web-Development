About Me

Biography

Formative Years

I grew up in Healdsburg, California—a quiet town surrounded by vineyards and redwoods. It was the kind of place where you could hear your own thoughts, and I spent a lot of time doing just that. I was always searching for something—some deeper meaning, some sense of direction. I didn’t know what it was yet, but I knew I wanted my life to matter.

Young Adult Years

When I moved to Santa Rosa for high school, things shifted. I graduated in 2009, and like a lot of people my age, I didn’t have a clear path laid out. So I enrolled at Santa Rosa Junior College and spent nearly a decade exploring different programs. I dabbled in everything—from music to tech to design—trying to figure out what truly resonated with me. At the same time, I worked a string of jobs, each one teaching me something new about myself, even if none of them felt like “the one.”

Responsible Years

Eventually, I found my calling in Information Technology. It clicked. I enrolled at Empire College and earned my Associate of Science in IT. That was a turning point. I started working in the industry and built a solid foundation over seven years. I was proud of the work I was doing, and for a while, it felt like I was finally on track.

The Lost Years

But life doesn’t always move in a straight line. Somewhere along the way, I lost myself. I got caught up in the party scene, started using cocaine, and before I knew it, it had taken over my life. What started as weekend fun became a daily habit. I was spiraling—my job was on the line, my health was deteriorating, and I felt completely disconnected from who I used to be.

I tried to escape by moving out of town for a fresh start. I managed to stay sober for about six months, but then I relapsed. The depression hit hard, and I fell back into the cycle. That’s when I made the hardest, most important, and best decision of my life: I checked into Touchstone Recovery Center in Fresno, California.

I spent 30 days in inpatient rehab, facing everything I had been running from. It wasn’t easy. But it was necessary. After that, I continued with aftercare, and slowly, I started rebuilding. As of September 21, 2025, I’ve been sober for 19 months. That number means everything to me. It’s not just time—it’s proof that I can change.

Present Day

Now, I’m enrolled at Grand Canyon University. I’m taking my life back, one step at a time. I’m not the same person I used to be—and I don’t want to be. I’ve learned that healing isn’t linear, and growth doesn’t come without pain. But I’m here. I’m present. And I’m building a future that reflects who I truly am.

Core Values

Integrity

Living in alignment with my principles even when no one is watching. Integrity means owning my choices, honoring my word, and staying grounded in truth, especially when it is inconvenient.

Resilience

The ability to rise after falling. Resilience is not just about surviving hardship—it is about transforming it into strength, insight, and forward motion. I have learned to bend without breaking.

Curiosity

A relentless drive to understand how things work—whether it is a broken system, a piece of lore, or the wiring behind a motherboard. Curiosity fuels my creativity and keeps me evolving.

Authenticity

I do not hide behind filters or polish. My story is raw, real, and mine. Authenticity means showing up as I am—flaws, growth, and all—and inviting others to do the same.

Discipline

The quiet force behind every breakthrough. Discipline is what gets me through late-night debugging, recovery milestones, and academic grind. It is the structure that supports my ambition.

Growth

I am not who I was—and I will not stay who I am. Growth is my commitment to becoming better, wiser, and more capable with every challenge I face. It is the heartbeat of my transformation.

Accountability

I own my actions, my past, and my future. Accountability means facing consequences, making amends, and building trust through consistency—not excuses.

Empathy

Understanding others is not just emotional—it is strategic. Empathy helps me build better systems, better relationships, and better communities. It is how I connect and collaborate.

Creativity

Whether I am designing wireframes or composing music, creativity is how I solve problems and express truth. It is the spark that turns logic into art and chaos into clarity.

Mastery

I do not settle for “good enough.” Mastery is my pursuit of excellence—through repetition, refinement, and obsession with detail. It is the difference between knowing and owning.

Community

I thrive in connection—whether it is a coding meetup, a recovery circle, or a shared playlist. Community gives my work meaning and my story context. I build with others in mind.

Courage

Courage is not loud, it is the quiet decision to keep going. It is walking into rehab, sharing my truth, and choosing growth over comfort. It is the backbone of every value I live by.

Disclaimer:

I definitely do not adhere to these core values with perfection, by any means. I strive to get as close as I can, making weekly reflections, so that I may make any necessary adjustments to my actions as needed.
